# When a refund is warranted

A refund is warranted when the customer was charged more than once for the same
order on the same day. The duplicate charge is refunded in full; the original
stands.

A refund is **not** warranted because the customer changed their mind, unless the
charge is less than 30 days old and the order has not shipped.

# Amounts

Never propose more than the charge itself. If two charges differ, refund the later
one — the earlier is the order of record.

# When to ask rather than decide

Ask if the ticket references a charge you cannot find, or if the customer's
description doesn't match what the charges show. A wrong refund costs more than a
question.
